Client Assets.
Accounted For.

Safeguarding client assets is a continuing operational responsibility. In the UK, the FCA’s Client Assets Sourcebook sets requirements for client money and safe custody assets.

CASS 7

Client Money

Applicable arrangements include segregation, records, reconciliations and acknowledgement arrangements with the banks holding client money. The statutory trust framework is designed to protect client money if a firm fails.

CASS 6

Safe Custody Assets

Applicable requirements cover safekeeping, ownership rights, records and reconciliations, helping distinguish client assets from the firm’s own assets and those of other clients.

DIFC

Separate client asset framework

EMFI Capital (DIFC) Limited is subject to the DFSA’s applicable client asset requirements. These are separate from the FCA’s CASS rules.

Segregation, records and reconciliations are designed to reduce specific custody and client asset risks. They do not eliminate investment losses, banking or depository failures, counterparty risk or every risk arising on insolvency.
