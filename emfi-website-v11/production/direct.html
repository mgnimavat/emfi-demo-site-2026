Direct Means Less.

Direct relationships are not about removing every intermediary. They are about reducing unnecessary layers, keeping responsibilities clear and giving clients a more transparent view of the operating chain.

Less Overlap.

A more direct operating structure can remove avoidable intermediary layers and repeated handling. Pricing depends on the service, asset, account and third-party costs.

Less Complexity.

Fewer handovers can make instructions easier to coordinate and exceptions easier to identify. The relevant service providers and market rules still apply.

Less Exposure.

For eligible clients, individually segregated depository accounts can keep securities separately identifiable rather than pooled with unrelated client holdings in an omnibus account.

Segregation can reduce certain cross-client operational and asset-identification risks. It does not eliminate market, counterparty, depository, banking or insolvency risk, and does not guarantee recovery in a default.

Direct Market
Access.

For eligible clients and supported instruments, EMFI can arrange Direct Market Access to selected trading venues through approved execution arrangements.

DMA allows approved clients to submit orders electronically through designated market access arrangements. Access is subject to client classification, onboarding, account size and trading activity criteria, venue and instrument availability, pre-trade controls, credit limits and ongoing monitoring. It is not unrestricted access and is assessed individually.
