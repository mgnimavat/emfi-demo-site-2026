Ten years.One connected chain.

Built for a different
kind of market.

EMFI began in specialist securities and emerging-market financial services. As client requirements evolved, so did the firm. Today the chain runs from intelligence to execution to settlement and custody, with funds and technology built on top. We work through specialist legal entities in the United Kingdom and the Dubai International Financial Centre, with fund structures in the Cayman Islands, for professional clients and eligible counterparties. Each service is provided by the relevant entity, within its permissions.

A decade of building the firm.

2016Establishment

Establishment

EMFI Securities Limited was incorporated on 26 October 2016, initially under the name SNB Securities Limited.

EMFI Securities

The company adopted the EMFI Securities name and began building its first institutional relationships.

Group structure

EMFI Capital Limited and EMFI Group Limited were incorporated in May 2018, giving the firm a holding company and a second operating entity.

UK investment and custody business

EMFI Capital Limited received FCA authorisation on 1 February 2019, and was registered as a small authorised UK AIFM on 29 October 2019.

Depository access

EMFI gained direct access to institutional settlement infrastructure, removing layers between clients and the depository.

Consolidating the model

Execution, settlement and custody continued to be built out as one connected operation, ahead of the move to proprietary technology.

Proprietary technology

EMFI launched its own client workflow platform: a Workstation for execution, a Dashboard for the account picture, and mobile access.

Growing the Intelligence offering

EMFI’s economists and strategists continued to widen coverage of emerging and frontier markets.

Fund structure

EMFI Fund SPC, a Cayman Islands segregated portfolio company, provides a common structure for distinct investment portfolios.

Dubai presence

EMFI Capital (DIFC) Limited was licensed by the DFSA on 30 July 2025.
