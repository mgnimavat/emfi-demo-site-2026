Not Everything
Fits a Dropdown.

Markets are not always standard. Client requirements are not always standard either.

A particular security, a cross-depository settlement route, a specific account structure or a time-sensitive instruction can require more than a standard workflow. EMFI starts by understanding the requirement, then assesses the available infrastructure, permissions, counterparties and operational constraints. Where a workable route exists, our people coordinate the details with you and the relevant providers.

Technology handles repeatable steps. Experienced people deal with exceptions, judgement and follow-through.

Every solution remains subject to applicable regulatory permissions, client eligibility, due diligence, sanctions screening, risk controls, market rules and third-party capabilities. EMFI does not undertake activity where those requirements cannot be met.
