Privacy notice.

Who we are. This website is operated by the EMFI group. The controller of your personal data is the EMFI group company that provides the service you ask about, as named under Regulatory information. Contact: contact@emfi.uk.

What we collect and why. Through the contact form: your name, company, work email, country and message, to reply to your enquiry. Through the Intelligence sign-up: the same details, your interests and your confirmation that you are a professional client or eligible counterparty, to send you EMFI Intelligence and to keep a record of that confirmation. We send Intelligence only with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who sees it. EMFI group companies and the service providers who host this site, send email and support our systems, under contract. We do not sell personal data.

Transfers and retention. Your data may be handled in the United Kingdom, the DIFC and the Cayman Islands, with appropriate safeguards where the law requires them. We keep it only as long as needed for these purposes or as the law requires.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to object to its use or to withdraw consent, by emailing us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies and storage. This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser stores one item for the length of your session: your confirmation on the fund page. The maps load only after you click “Load map”; the map is then provided by Google, which receives your IP address.

Accessibility.

We aim for this website to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. If you find a barrier, or need information in another format, email contact@emfi.uk and tell us the page and the problem. Maps are provided by Google and may not meet the same standard.
