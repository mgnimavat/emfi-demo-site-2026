Not Cross Settle.

International securities may need to move between different depositories. Securities belonging to different clients should not cross between unrelated settlement obligations.

In a shared omnibus account, securities bought for one client can be used to meet another client’s delivery. For larger clients, we can open dedicated Euroclear accounts so your securities stay identified as yours.

Cross-depository settlement

For supported securities and markets, we can also settle across depositories: a seller settling through DTC, for example, can deliver to a buyer receiving through Euroclear. Route, eligibility and charges depend on the security and the counterparties.

Mind the
(Settlement) Gap.

When your buy settles but your corresponding sell fails to settle, you can be left financing the position and paying interest while you wait.

We link your buy to your sell. If the sell is not ready to settle, the buy is held back, so you are not left financing the position. CSDR penalties and TMPG charges can still apply. Where they do, we pass them through with the references you need to review and dispute them.

The buy has settled while the onward sell is not ready. The position may need to be financed in the interim.

With linked trades

Sell instruction

The linked buy is not released independently while the corresponding sell is unable to settle, subject to the relevant infrastructure and instruction conditions.

Linked settlement can mitigate the financing position described above. It does not prevent all settlement failures or remove CSDR, TMPG or other applicable fail charges.
