A Fund Is More
Than a Portfolio.

Investment strategy is one part of a fund. The legal structure, portfolio arrangements, administration, audit and legal oversight are part of the operating framework too.

EMFI Fund SPC is a Cayman Islands segregated portfolio company designed to accommodate distinct investment portfolios. Each portfolio operates under its applicable investment mandate and governing documents, with the relevant service-provider appointments and arrangements set out in the fund documentation.

The Structure Behind
the Strategy.

Wealth management begins with the investment mandate, not a standard product. The portfolio needs to reflect the client’s objectives, risk parameters, liquidity requirements and constraints.

01

Define the mandate

Establish the investment objective, time horizon, liquidity needs and permitted instruments.

02

Construct and manage

Mandates informed by EMFI’s own market intelligence, with execution, settlement and custody in the same group.

03

Monitor and review

Review the portfolio against its agreed parameters, risk framework and reporting requirements.

The nature of investment management services, decision-making responsibility and client eligibility depend on the relevant agreement, legal entity and regulatory permissions.
