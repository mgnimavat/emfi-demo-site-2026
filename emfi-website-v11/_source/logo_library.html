A single visual directory of third-party identities discussed for the EMFI website. Logos are grouped by their intended page context. Each card links to its image source or official brand-resource page.

Use note. This is a reference board, not a grant of trademark or brand-use rights. Most previews are remotely hosted reference images, not local master artwork. CISI says the firm brandmark contains a year range and is updated annually. Do not reuse another firm's badge or an expired mark.

Items to keep out of this logo wall

FCA, DFSA and CIMA: use regulator names, exact entity disclosures and official register links, not their logos. Avoid any suggestion of endorsement.

Bloomberg: treat EMF newswire / EMFI<GO> as a separate market-presence reference, not an affiliation or service-provider relationship. Use Bloomberg marks only with permission.

Media publications: add only where there is a specific, verifiable article, quotation or interview. Link each outlet mark to the underlying coverage.

Tradegate / Börse Berlin: the two exchanges merged into Tradegate Berlin Stock Exchange on 1 January 2026. Confirm the exact EMFI venue relationship before deciding whether to use the current Tradegate BSX mark or refer to the legacy Börse Berlin/Equiduct relationship.
