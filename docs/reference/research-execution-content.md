Investment decisions are only useful when they can be translated into execution. EMFI combines market research with professional execution, giving clients a direct relationship from insight to trade.

## 7.2 · Execution

## 7.3 · Why research and execution belong together

FROM VIEW TO ACTION.

The value of research is amplified when the same relationship can help translate an investment view into a completed transaction. This integrated workflow reduces unnecessary handoffs between research, sales and execution.

### The IMF Discount

- ID: venezuela-imf-discount
- Country: Venezuela
- Report type: Fixed Income
- Published: 2026-09-28
- Topics: Country Report, Quantitative Insight

Read supplied excerpt

Bitácora Económica reported that the Rodríguez administration has decided to delay the debt restructuring and that the IMF might become involved in producing the DSA framework.

We put numbers on our view that IMF involvement would keep stringent sustainability constraints, use a far more conservative oil production path and extend the restructuring timeline.

The IMF scenario cuts the NPV/Face of the restructuring package by 18.8 pts to 37.9%, against 56.7% without IMF involvement.

Source context: Supplied EMFI research screenshots: report selector and readable report excerpt. Publication date is the date displayed in the screenshot; this is historical source text, not a current recommendation.
