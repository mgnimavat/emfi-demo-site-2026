02 / CUSTODY

Custody is a responsibility, not a feature.

Holding client money and securities comes with specific obligations.

Our UK custody and safeguarding arrangements operate within the FCA's Client Assets Sourcebook (CASS).

CASS 7 | Client Money

Client money held or controlled by EMFI Capital Limited is subject to applicable segregation requirements. These include appropriate records, reconciliations and arrangements with the banks holding client money.

The statutory trust framework is designed to protect client money in the event of a firm's failure.

CASS 6 | Safe Custody Assets

Safe custody assets are subject to separate requirements governing their safekeeping, records and reconciliations.

These rules are designed to protect clients' ownership rights and help distinguish client assets from those belonging to the firm or other clients.

Protection through process. Segregation, accurate records, reconciliations and oversight are fundamental to the way custody arrangements operate.

Our wider control framework and regulatory oversight are explained on the Dependable Operations page.

EMFI Capital Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA and is subject to the applicable CASS requirements in respect of client money and safe custody assets it holds or controls.

EMFI Capital (DIFC) Limited is separately regulated by the DFSA and subject to the applicable DIFC client asset requirements.

Client asset protections do not eliminate all custody, banking or third-party risks and do not protect against investment losses.
