02 / ASSET MANAGEMENT

A fund is more than a portfolio.

An investment strategy needs more than capital and an investment manager.

It requires a proper legal structure, defined responsibilities and professional oversight.

EMFI Fund SPC is a Cayman Islands segregated portfolio company providing a common fund structure for distinct investment portfolios.

Each segregated portfolio has its own investment mandate, assets and applicable portfolio-level arrangements, subject to the company's constitutional documents and relevant offering materials.

Fund structure information

EMFI Fund SPC is established in the Cayman Islands as a segregated portfolio company and operates within the applicable Cayman Islands regulatory framework.

The structure supports distinct investment portfolios, each subject to its respective investment mandate, governing documents, appointed service providers and eligibility requirements.

Fund investments involve risk, including possible loss of capital. The relevant offering documents contain the full details of each portfolio and its investment terms.
